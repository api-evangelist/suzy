# Suzy – Real‑Time, AI‑Driven Consumer Insights Platform
> Suzy is an AI‑driven research ecosystem that unifies quant, qual, and proprietary audiences so brands can ask more, guess less, and move at the speed of culture.

## Platform Products
- [Why Suzy](https://www.suzy.com/why-suzy): Overview of Suzy’s unified, AI‑driven research approach.
- [Suzy Insights](https://www.suzy.com/suzy-insights): Build surveys and get AI summaries & crosstabs in minutes.
- [Suzy Live](https://www.suzy.com/suzy-live): Moderated IDIs & focus groups with auto transcripts and highlight reels.
- [Suzy Speaks](https://www.suzy.com/suzy-speaks): AI‑moderated voice interviews for rich qualitative feedback at scale.
- [Suzy Signals](https://www.suzy.com/suzy-signals): Trend intelligence that links research to real‑time cultural shifts.
- [Suzy Audiences](https://www.suzy.com/suzy-audiences): On‑demand access to 1 M+ verified consumers, protected by BIOTIC fraud detection.
- [AI Suite](https://www.suzy.com/ai-suite): Screeners, summaries, themeing, predictive heatmaps, and more.
- [Suzy Enterprise](https://www.suzy.com/suzy-enterprise): Governance, unlimited seats, enterprise‑grade support.

## Example Scenarios
> **Suzy Insights:** A CPG team tests three packaging concepts with 400 shoppers, gets AI summaries, and chooses the winner in 24 hours.  
> **Suzy Live:** A UX team conducts five remote IDIs, bookmarks pain‑points, and compiles a highlight reel for designers overnight.  
> **Suzy Speaks:** A beverage brand runs 200 AI voice interviews, uncovering flavor preferences 4× faster than traditional qual.  
> **Suzy Signals:** A marketing strategist tracks a rising hydration trend and receives weekly alerts plus suggested survey questions.  
> **Suzy Audiences:** A finance app re‑contacts a custom segment of Gen Z investors for follow‑up questions two weeks after launch.
